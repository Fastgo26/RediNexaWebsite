Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about RediNexa.

What is RediNexa?
RediNexa is an online platform that brings together travel services, useful products and selected online offers in one place.
Can I book flights through RediNexa?
RediNexa provides links to selected travel partners where visitors can search and book flights.
What is a travel eSIM?
A travel eSIM is a digital SIM that can help you stay connected while travelling without needing a physical SIM card.
Does RediNexa sell the products directly?
No. Product links may take you to third-party shopping platforms. Purchases are completed on those external websites.
Does RediNexa earn money from affiliate links?
Yes. Some links on RediNexa are affiliate links. RediNexa may receive a commission when a purchase or booking is made through certain links.
Does using an affiliate link cost more?
Affiliate commissions do not normally add an additional cost to the customer. Prices are determined by the external service or shopping platform.
How can I contact RediNexa?
You can contact RediNexa through the Contact page available in the website footer.
