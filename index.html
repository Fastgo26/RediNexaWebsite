What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. They can help a website remember settings, understand how visitors use the site, and provide relevant services.

How RediNexa may use cookies

  • Necessary technologies: used for basic website operation, security and navigation.
  • Analytics: if analytics tools are enabled, they may collect information about how visitors use the website.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense and other advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies when advertising is displayed.
  • Affiliate links: affiliate partners may use cookies or similar technologies to attribute a referral or commission.

Third-party services

RediNexa may link to or use services provided by third parties, including travel providers, product platforms, affiliate networks, analytics services and advertising providers. Their own privacy and cookie policies apply to their services.

Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Where consent is required, RediNexa will provide appropriate choices before placing non-essential cookies or similar technologies.
