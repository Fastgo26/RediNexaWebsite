How to Plan a Trip: 10 Checks to Make Before You Book

Planning a trip is easier when you separate the exciting part—choosing where to go—from the practical checks that protect your time and budget. This guide gives you a simple checklist you can use before paying for a flight, accommodation, transfer or activity.

1. Confirm your travel dates

Write down the exact departure and return dates before you compare offers. If your dates are flexible, check more than one nearby date. A small change can affect the total cost, travel time and availability.

2. Check the departure and arrival airports

Two flights to the same city may use different airports. Check the airport name and how you will reach your accommodation after landing. Include local transport in your budget, especially when an airport is far from the city centre.

3. Look at the complete price

The first price you see is not always the final cost. Check baggage, seat selection, payment charges, airport transfers and other optional services. Compare the total amount rather than comparing only the headline price.

4. Read the baggage rules

Baggage allowances differ between airlines and ticket types. Check the number of bags, weight limits and cabin-bag dimensions before you travel. This can help you avoid unexpected charges at the airport.

5. Check cancellation and change conditions

Before payment, look for the rules covering cancellations, refunds and date changes. Some bookings are flexible while others are not. Keep a copy of the conditions that apply to the booking you choose.

6. Check your travel documents

Make sure your passport or other travel document will be valid for the journey and check whether your destination requires a visa or other authorization. Requirements can depend on nationality, residence status and destination, so use official government information for the final check.

7. Plan your internet connection

If you need mobile internet abroad, check whether your phone supports eSIM and whether it is unlocked. Compare coverage, data allowance, validity and activation instructions. If you choose an eSIM, read the installation instructions before leaving home.

8. Plan the first trip from the airport

Decide how you will get from the airport to your accommodation. Compare public transport, taxi services and pre-booked transfers. Consider your arrival time, luggage and whether the transport option operates when your flight lands.

9. Keep your important booking information together

Save your booking reference, accommodation address, transport details and important contact information somewhere you can access them while travelling. Keeping these details together makes it easier to solve problems if your phone connection is limited.

10. Recheck everything before payment

Take one final minute to check the passenger name, dates, airport, destination, baggage, total price and cancellation conditions. A final review can catch simple mistakes before the transaction is completed.

RediNexa tip: Use this checklist every time you plan a trip. Travel offers, prices, schedules and provider rules can change, so always confirm the latest details with the relevant provider before making a booking.
